# Privacy Policy: Quran No Ads

**Effective date:** 24 November 2026

Quran No Ads ("the app") is a free IndoPak 13-line Quran app published by Azee studio. It has no ads, no
purchases and no accounts, and it is not for sale.

## Summary

**The app does not collect, send or share any personal data.** It does not use the internet at all: the Android app does
not even ask for internet permission.

## What is stored, and where

To work, the app saves a few settings **on your phone only**:

- the page where you stopped reading (your "Continue reading" place) and recently read surahs;
- your bookmarks;
- your theme (Light, Sepia or Night);
- whether the daily reminder is on, and its time;
- whether the welcome screens have been shown;
- measured line widths, so pages open faster.

This information never leaves your phone. The developer cannot see it. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear
its data.

## Permissions

- **Notifications:** only to show the daily reading reminder, if you turn it on. The reminder is scheduled on your phone;
  no server is involved. You can turn it off at any time with the bell on the Home screen or in your phone settings.
- **Run at startup** (Android): so the reminder still works after your phone restarts.
- **Keep the screen on:** only while the Quran page is open, so the screen does not turn off while you read.

## What the app does not do

- No ads and no advertising IDs.
- No analytics, crash reporting or tracking.
- No accounts, sign-in or contact lists.
- No location, camera, microphone, photos or files.
- No data is sold or shared with anyone.

## Children

The app is suitable for readers of all ages. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Email

If you choose to report a mistake by email from the About page, your phone's email app opens a message to the developer.
That message, and the address it is sent from, are only used to reply to you and fix the mistake.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date.
