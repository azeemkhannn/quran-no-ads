# Code of Conduct

This project serves the Book of Allah, so we ask everyone who takes part to behave with the adab (good
manners) the Quran teaches: honesty, patience, kindness and respect.

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a welcoming place for everyone, whatever their background, school of thought,
nationality, age, level of experience or language. We will treat each other with respect, and we will
keep discussions focused on making the app better for its readers.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and people whose first language is not English.
- Give and accept feedback on the work, not the person.
- Assume good intentions. Ask before you accuse.
- Treat the Quran and religious matters with respect. This project is not a place for debates between
  schools of thought or sects.
- Credit other people's work, and respect the owners of the font and the Quran data.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Insults, harassment, mockery or personal attacks
- Disrespect towards the Quran, Islam, or anyone's faith or background
- Sectarian arguments or attempts to push changes to the Quran text without a verified source
- Spam, advertising, or attempts to add ads, tracking or paid features to the app
- Sharing anyone's private information without their permission

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues or pull requests, and block people who break
this code of conduct.

To report a problem, email the maintainer, **Azeem Ullah Khan**, at **azeemkhannnn7@gmail.com**. Reports
are kept private.

This code of conduct is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.
